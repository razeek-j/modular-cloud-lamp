# V05 — superseded lighting experiments

**Historical only. Use [the V06 lighting study](../lighting-v06/README.md) for the current geometry.**

V05 tried selected straight LED trays concealed in individual folds. The initial six-tray / 1.5 m / 12 W proposal and `01-led-placement-and-tray.png` are superseded drafts; they do not describe the final saved V05 geometry. Later drafts shortened trays into 100 mm modules grouped in five pockets (1.3 m / 10.4 W). Bare-source concealment did not conceal the tray bodies and luminous slots. Several bodies were visible as bars in normal views. That failed the intended appearance.

The V05 Blender files, source, intermediate renders and checkpoints remain preserved for inspection. Their electrical/component arrangements were not physically tested. V06 moves the tape into a separate ceiling mixing deck instead.
