# V17 against the reference photo: measured differences

Made 16 November 2026 at the user's request. [Side-by-side image](Reference-comparison.png): the reference on top, V17 below, both on the same 100-pixel grid. V17 was rendered from the reference's estimated camera, about 17° below the ceiling plane with the lamp filling the frame. In the reference, 1 pixel is about 1 mm (the lamp spans 1,152 px for a stated 1,200 mm). Reference numbers are read off the photo and are estimates. V17 numbers come from the model.

| **Edge waves** | Top and bottom edges rise and fall about ±50 mm, wavelength about 300 mm | Edges level through the middle, waves only at the rim | Mostly missing |

## Targets for the next version

- **Stance:** upright for 65–75% of the length; flat only at the crossings, for at most about 12%.
- **Width:** 150 mm, nearly constant (140–160 mm), so the broad faces come from the ribbon itself. Depth then lands near 165 mm.
- **Three curve speeds only:** slow sweeps (radius 500 mm or more, 350–550 mm long); hairpin turns (centreline radius 100–115 mm, turning 190–220°); eased transitions of 80–120 mm between them. Nothing tighter than 60 mm radius anywhere.
- **Hairpins:** five or six around the outline at varied distances from the centre, leaning 20–30° so their lit inside faces show.
- **Twist:** one half-twist per 300–350 mm, centred on each crossing: flat exactly where the ribbons pass, upright mid-span.
- **Edge waves:** ±45–55 mm at a wavelength of about 300 mm, with slopes up to about 45°.
- **Layers:** four ribbon passes through the middle, three at the edges.
- **Light:** strips placed to light the ribbon faces inside the loops, as well as the ceiling.
- **Support:** carried at the crossings and under the disc, with no arms showing.
- **Round outline:** wrap this band language around a circle, with the ribbons running mainly around the lamp and not across its centre.

## Curls and widths, spot by spot

Added 16 November 2026. [Annotated reference](Reference-curls-and-widths.png): blue bars are ribbon widths as seen, red circles are curls, green diamonds are twist nodes. Widths are read in pixels (about 1 px per mm). A ribbon turned partly edge-on looks narrower than it is, so each seen width is a lower bound on the true width.

There are four curl speeds: lips at 45–50 mm, end loops at 92–100 mm, waves at 155–210 mm, and near-straight sweeps above 500 mm. V17 has only one, a medium curve of 208–445 mm. It has no end loops and no lips.

Three patterns:

- **Swell and neck.** Each span is widest in its middle and narrowest at the twist nodes. Part of the narrowing at a node is the ribbon turning edge-on; the true width there is probably about 90–100 mm.
- **Hero in the middle.** The widest ribbon (about 150–165 mm) is in the centre. Widths step down toward the ends, to about 105 mm at the right loop.
- **Front ribbon tapers.** The lower front ribbon steps 108, 93, 75 from left to right.

The true width therefore ranges from about 95 to 165 mm, a ratio near 1 : 1.7.

### Added targets for the next version

- **Width rule:** each span swells to its full width at mid-span and necks to about 95 mm at each twist node, with a smooth, single-peaked profile.
- **Width grading:** hero ribbon 160 mm at its widest, second ribbon 135 mm, third 115 mm; each tapers about 25% toward its end loops.
- **End loops:** radius 90–100 mm, turning 195–230°, leaning 20–45°.
- **Lips:** one or two rolled lips of 45–55 mm radius where the widest faces turn under. Never tighter than 40 mm.
- **Waves:** crests and bellies of 150–210 mm radius, 230–280 mm apart on the front ribbon and 310–370 mm on the upper one.
