# V11 rolled ribbon revision

[Open the rolled Blender model](Ribbon-Lamp-V11-Rolled.blend) · [Room angle](05-Room-angle.png) · [Directly below](01-Underside.png) · [Low ceiling angle](02-Low-angle.png)

This revision rolls the ribbon faces across their width. The broad right fold turns over at its rim, the left turn is rounder, and the lower sweep curls back up. The centre cover is recessed above the front layers. The plan remains asymmetric and shallow, with a **1,211 × 1,057 mm** footprint and **159 mm maximum drop**. The approved images are packed in the Blender file.

| Cross-section through the bend | Initial depth across width | Rolled depth across width |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Left turn | 34 mm | 102 mm |
| Broad right turn | 35 mm | 121 mm |
| Lower sweep | 23 mm | 93 mm |

These values are section depths across the ribbon face at selected matching design stations. Wall thickness remains nominally 1.6 mm. The placement study still uses three continuous meshes. Shape and light distribution remain development work against the approved concept.

The three skins passed self-intersection, pairwise and canopy intersection checks. The right overlap has about **18 mm** of vertical clearance at the centre of a sampled **66 × 26 mm** patch. A single trial 100 mm LED channel is tucked between the folds. It passed all 150 sampled housing sightlines. The three camera sweeps found zero first hits on its housing or tape. A visible soft shadow remains on the lit curve. [Geometry and light checks](Development-checks.json) · [Section and camera comparisons](Room-view-check.json)

The separate full-size right-joint bench scene remains in the file. The captive profile still needs to be fitted to the revised overlap. The centre seats, final wiring and print cuts follow after that fit. Physical fit, heat and overhead retention still require bench validation.
